FESTIVAL THEME

UPON THE 'BE-CAUSE'

✦

Art does not merely mirror reality; it questions, awakens, and redeems. "Upon the 'Be-Cause'" is an ode to purpose-driven expression—a philosophical reflection on the reasons and convictions that ignite the creative spirit. At Kerala Sahityotsav 2026, every brushstroke, spoken verse, melody, and dramatic gesture becomes a deliberate response to the world around us. We celebrate creativity not merely for celebration's sake, but for the profound 'cause' that gives every human expression its timeless soul.

"Every stroke carries a question, every rhythm echoes a purpose."
